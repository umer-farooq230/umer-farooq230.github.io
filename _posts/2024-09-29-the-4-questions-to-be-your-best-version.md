<p>This is a breakdown of the video by <a href="https://youtu.be/-oXigF0frLE">Clark Kegley</a> and my addition to these questions</p><p>In this world, although we have an equal amount of time some people have really different results. The results that everyone wants. This is not about time management and how to make a calendar and color code. Nope</p><p><strong>Productivity. </strong>The go-to word for every influencer. Clark says that most productivity tips just tell us how to be busy. Real Productivity doesn’t have to be busy. It’s about getting the work done which really matters and affects your life.</p><h3>Your Life</h3><p>This is the graph Clark talks about in the video. This is a normal life chart and how normal people traverse through this period of life leads to misery and guilt</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*DRqRBGVHa4pV4z0bJkWK5w.jpeg" /></figure><p>How a normal person will go through life is this:</p><p>Soak up every piece of knowledge they can in the <strong>young phase</strong>. Do that trending course or degree, Go to college (if they are smart), Do that internship in a big company. Get hired by that company. And then be ready for the <strong>Adult Phase. </strong>Now from here climb the cooperate ladder and do robotic work for the next 40 years to save up for the family and retirement. After entering the <strong>Old Phase,</strong> it is just wait and wait till someone buries your body</p><p>(Living a normal life isn’t bad but being a burden on yourself is)</p><h4>Living or Preparing to Live</h4><p>Watching videos about Productivity or reading books isn’t living but preparing to live. There should be a point in your life where you go out there, have some failures and Live</p><p>Most people have this idea in mind “When I retire, I will have money and freedom and only then I can truly live the life”.</p><p>The truth is, if you don&#39;t enjoy the Adult phase, you are missing life, because in that part of life, you have optimistic health and time and freedom</p><h3>The 4 Questions</h3><p>Clark gives these amazing questions and if you answer these 4 questions honestly, it will change your perspective of seeing things</p><h4>1. What would you do first if you were fearless and could not fail?</h4><p>Most of the time we don&#39;t start something because we are scared. Remember a time when you had a great idea but somehow you just shot it down? If you have seen someone starting a business and you have an idea of how to do that business in a better way, a flood of negative thoughts will be in your mind. “It&#39;s a waste of time”. “I am very busy for that”. “This is not going to work”.</p><p>That is because of fear of failure. So answer this question with honesty and see what results you get</p><h4>2. What will I be most proud of 1 year from today?</h4><p>Clark is talking about being proud of your character. Making plans and everything is one thing but giving them your time and energy is another thing and this is what you have to focus. Think of this concept in reverse order.</p><p>Will you be proud that you fixed your health? Or getting out of that crappy job? Or starting that business and taking that leap?</p><p>And this <strong>Does not mean </strong>that you start to hate talk to yourself in the mirror and force yourself to do all that work. This will burn you out in no time and you will have done nothing.</p><h4>3. What are the 3 things that I need to stop doing?</h4><p>Achieving success is not only pursuing it with all your might. It is also about keeping track of the things that come in the way and distract you. These distractions are taking your time and energy that you don&#39;t even know about. And this can demotivate you out of your goals</p><p>Can’t start that business? Try to stop spending on useless things. Can’t lose the weight? Track your calories and stop binge eating.</p><p>This is the most important question because we often neglect that there are some habits that we have to leave to become the better self</p><h4>4. What advice would I give someone if they were in my situation?</h4><p>Humans are great at giving advice but horrible when it comes to act upon it. If you are lost and don&#39;t know what to do, take a step back and put someone else in your shoes.</p><p>Where did they go wrong? Which was the wrong step that lead to this situation? Now it has been done, what now?</p><p>Human mind is one incredible thing and we know it when something is wrong. So not being you for sometime can bring solutions to your mind you would have never thought were possible</p><p>Hope you learned something. Let me know your thoughts :)</p><p>— Umer out</p><img src="https://medium.com/_/stat?event=post.clientViewed&referrerSource=full_rss&postId=15b6e6769af2" width="1" height="1" alt="">
